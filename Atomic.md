General notes, when talking about "stability" here we are referring to entropy, Lithiums move for example that has a bonus effect if the stability is at 100% is the same
as saying entropy is at 0%. For a move that restores stability by 5% for example, this would lower global entropy by 5%. Thank you for your attention to this matter.
